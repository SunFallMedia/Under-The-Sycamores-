Core Mission

Beneath the canopy of the Ohio Valley lies a history of forgotten cases and untold truths — investigated by father-daughter hosts Jeremy and Kayla.

How we work

Every case is built on primary documents: FOIA files, court records, and incident reports — not sensational speculation. By combining structural data analysis with human-centered digital research, we investigate unresolved crimes, political corruption, and bizarre regional mysteries throughout Kentucky, Indiana, and Ohio.

Tone

Grounded, analytical, and persistent — a father-daughter partnership, respectful of the people at the center of every case, and objective about the institutions that failed them.

Who we cover

A compelling case is a compelling case, full stop. We choose cases because they deserve answers — not because the victim fits a demographic true crime has historically favored. Race, economic status, and sexual orientation have no bearing on whether a story gets told here.

Who's Behind the Mic

A father-daughter investigation, told from two angles.

Jeremy

Co-Host & Lead Analyst

With over 12 years of experience as an engineering technician in civil construction, Jeremy brings a rigorous, data-driven approach to Under the Sycamores. Rather than coming from a traditional journalism background, he relies on mathematical logic, structural data analysis, and technical literacy to dissect case files. A lifelong Midwest resident with a deep familiarity with the region, Jeremy turned a passion for true crime into an objective search for answers—focusing on raw facts, documented timelines, and hard evidence over speculation.

Kayla

Co-Host & Digital Researcher

Born and raised in Kentucky, Kayla brings a compassionate, human-centered lens to Under the Sycamores. Currently studying cybersecurity, she combines digital investigative skills with a deep understanding of how modern cases unfold online. While analyzing raw data and official records is essential, Kayla ensures the show never loses sight of the human stories behind the files—focusing on victim advocacy, empathetic storytelling, and a fresh perspective on the Ohio Valley's cold cases.
